Yes, Healix can be used without wearables. You can manually log your health data or use your smartphone's sensors.

Doctor chat support allows you to consult with licensed health professionals directly through the app.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later? +

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time from your account settings with no extra fees.

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required to get started.
