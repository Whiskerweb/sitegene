About Northpeak

Simple answers to make things clear.

Is the platform suitable for beginners?＋

Absolutely. Northpeak ships with a guided setup, sensible defaults and plain-language insights, so you don't need a data team to get value on day one.

How accurate are the AI insights?＋

Our models are continuously evaluated against your historical outcomes. You always see the confidence level behind each recommendation, so you stay in control.

Can I integrate with my existing tools?＋

Yes. Northpeak connects to the platforms you already use in seconds, with no engineering work required for the most common integrations.

What security measures do you have?＋

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access controls and audit logging. Your data is never used to train shared models.

How often are insights updated?＋

Dashboards refresh in near real-time as new data arrives, and strategic insights are recalculated on a rolling schedule throughout the day.

What kind of support do you offer?＋

Every plan includes support. Advanced and Enterprise plans add priority and 24/7 channels, plus a dedicated specialist for onboarding.
