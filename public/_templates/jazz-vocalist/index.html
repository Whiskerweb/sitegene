About

A London-based jazz and soul vocalist with over a decade performing in some of the city's most respected rooms. Her voice moves comfortably across the Great American Songbook, contemporary jazz standards and soul classics, a repertoire broad enough to suit any room, always delivered with the same attention.

She performs solo with piano accompaniment, as a duo with guitar or with her full quartet, and has built a reputation for sets that feel tailored rather than rehearsed. Every booking begins with a conversation about the evening, the space, the guests, the atmosphere. The music follows from there and forward.
